Job Title: Senior Software Engineer (Payment Infrastructure)
Company: Stripe
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote

About the Role:
At Stripe, we build financial infrastructure that powers millions of businesses worldwide. We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our Core Payment Routing & Reliability team. You will design, build, and operate foundational distributed systems that handle billions of dollars in daily transaction volume with strict zero-downtime tolerance.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, scale, and maintain high-throughput backend services and public-facing APIs with 99.999% reliability.
- Build fault-tolerant distributed systems capable of handling unexpected upstream banking gateway failures with automated idempotent retries.
- Optimize database performance, connection pooling, and caching across high-volume relational and distributed storage systems.
- Collaborate with security, platform, and product teams to safeguard financial telemetry and user transactions.
- Participate in operational on-call rotations and lead post-incident root cause analyses (RCAs).

Requirements & Qualifications:
- 5+ years of software engineering experience delivering production backend systems at scale.
- Proficiency in Python, Go, Java, or Ruby, with a strong understanding of concurrency, event loops, and asynchronous I/O.
- Deep hands-on experience with SQL databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL), distributed caching (Redis/Memcached), and message brokers (Kafka/RabbitMQ).
- Strong track record in system architecture, capacity planning, and microservices reliability patterns.
- Excellent communication and cross-functional leadership skills.
