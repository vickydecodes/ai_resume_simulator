Job Title: Investment Banking Associate (Technology, Media & Telecom)
Company: Goldman Sachs
Location: New York, NY

About Goldman Sachs:
Goldman Sachs is a leading global investment banking, securities, and investment management firm providing financial services to corporations, financial institutions, and governments.

Role Overview:
As an Investment Banking Associate in our Global TMT Group, you will play an integral role in advising leading technology enterprises on mergers and acquisitions (M&A), equity and debt financings, and corporate restructuring.

Responsibilities:
- Build, audit, and manage complex financial models including DCF, LBO, three-statement operating models, and merger consequence analyses.
- Synthesize industry market trends, valuation multiples, and competitive benchmarking into executive pitch books.
- Lead due diligence streams, coordinating directly with client C-suite executives, legal counsel, and accounting teams.
- Manage and mentor investment banking analysts on modeling standards and valuation rigor.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of investment banking, private equity, or top-tier management consulting experience.
- Mastery of financial accounting, corporate valuation frameworks (DCF, comparable companies, precedent transactions, LBO), and M&A dynamics.
- Exceptional analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving abilities.
- High standards of precision, executive communication, and team leadership.
