Job Title: Enterprise Account Executive (Sales Cloud)
Company: Salesforce
Location: New York, NY / Remote

About Salesforce:
Salesforce is the global leader in CRM and enterprise cloud solutions, empowering companies of every size and industry to connect with their customers in a whole new way.

Role Overview:
As an Enterprise Account Executive on our Strategic Accounts team, you will drive new business revenue across enterprise organizations. You will own the full sales lifecycle from prospecting to executive presentation, contract negotiation, and closing large-scale multi-year contracts.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead multi-threaded enterprise sales cycles, managing relationships from VP to C-level decision makers (CIO, CMO, CFO, CEO).
- Apply rigorous qualification frameworks (MEDDPICC) to forecast revenue accurately and maintain a healthy 4x+ rolling pipeline.
- Conduct tailored, value-based business discovery to uncover strategic pains and quantify ROI impact.
- Orchestrate cross-functional account teams including Solutions Engineers, Customer Success Architects, Legal, and Product Specialists.
- Negotiate complex commercial and master services agreements (MSAs) ensuring mutually beneficial long-term partnerships.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of direct quota-carrying experience in enterprise B2B SaaS software sales.
- Proven track record of consistently exceeding annual quota targets ($1.5M+ ARR targets) with average deal sizes over $100K.
- Mastery of modern sales methodologies (MEDDIC/MEDDPICC, Challenger, Command of the Message).
- Exceptional executive presence, commercial acumen, and consultative negotiation skills.
- Familiarity with enterprise CRM workflows, data governance, and cloud business models.
